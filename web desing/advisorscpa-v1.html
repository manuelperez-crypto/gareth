01 About the firm
2026
Joined the DSF group

A neighborhood CPA firm, with the resources of a group.

Advisors CPA has served individuals, families, and closely-held businesses from our Midland Park office — the kind of practice where your accountant knows your name, your history, and what changed since last year.

In 2026, Advisors CPA became part of the DSF group. Day to day, nothing changes about how we treat clients. What changes is what stands behind us: a deeper bench, a full resource library, and a team whose experience runs from bookkeeping to CFO-level strategy and SEC-regulated fund operations.

We now serve as the dedicated tax practice for every DSF client — and DSF's capabilities are available to every Advisors CPA client who needs them.

"Small-firm attention. Group-level depth."
02 Services

Everything a year of good numbers requires.

From the April deadline to the everyday bookkeeping behind it — one team, under one roof, all year long.

01

Individual Tax Preparation

Accurate, on-time personal returns — prepared by a CPA team that looks for what software alone misses.

  • Federal and state individual returns (Form 1040)
  • Multi-state and part-year resident returns
  • Rental properties, investments, and K-1 income
  • Amended and prior-year returns
  • Electronic filing and direct deposit
02

Business Tax Preparation

Corporate and partnership returns built on clean books — filed right the first time, every deadline met.

  • Corporate returns (1120, 1120-S) and partnerships (1065)
  • Schedule K-1 preparation for owners and partners
  • State and local business filings
  • Sales and use tax returns
  • 1099 preparation and filing
03

Tax Planning & Strategy

The return is the last step. The savings happen in the planning — done all year, not in April.

  • Year-round planning and projections
  • Quarterly estimated payment calculations
  • Retirement and deferral strategies
  • Income and deduction timing
  • Life-event planning — sale of a home, equity comp, inheritance
04

IRS & State Representation

A letter from the IRS is not a crisis — it's a process. We stand between you and it, start to finish.

  • IRS and state notice response
  • Audit representation and support
  • Payment plans and installment agreements
  • Penalty abatement requests
  • Back-year filings and catch-up work
05

Bookkeeping & Accounting

Clean, current books — so tax time is a review, not an archaeology project.

  • Monthly bookkeeping and reconciliations
  • QuickBooks setup, cleanup, and support
  • Financial statements and month-end close
  • Accounts payable and receivable support
  • Audit-ready records, all year
06

Payroll Services

People get paid correctly and on time — and every filing behind the paycheck is handled.

  • Payroll processing and direct deposit
  • Federal and state payroll tax filings
  • W-2 and 1099 year-end preparation
  • New-hire reporting and compliance
  • Payroll setup for new businesses
07

Entity Selection & New Business

Start the business on the right structure — the decision that quietly shapes every tax year after.

  • Entity selection — LLC, S-corp, C-corp, partnership
  • Formation filings and registrations
  • S-election preparation (Form 2553)
  • EIN, payroll, and sales tax registrations
  • First-year accounting setup
08

Estates, Trusts & Gift Tax

Careful, compassionate work for the returns nobody plans to need — done right when it matters most.

  • Fiduciary income tax returns (Form 1041)
  • Estate and gift tax returns (Forms 706 / 709)
  • Trust accounting support
  • Coordination with estate attorneys

04 How it works

Four steps. No surprises.

The same process whether it's a single 1040 or a business with payroll and quarterly filings.

01

Send us your documents

Drop them off at the office, mail them, or send them digitally — we'll give you a simple checklist so nothing gets missed.

02

We prepare — and double-review

Your return is prepared by the team and reviewed before anything is filed. Questions come to you early, not the night before the deadline.

03

Review it together

We walk you through the result in plain language — what you owe or get back, what changed from last year, and why.

04

File, then plan ahead

We e-file, confirm acceptance, and flag what to do differently next year — so every season starts better than the last one.
