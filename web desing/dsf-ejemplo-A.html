01 About the firm
2020
Founded · Hackensack NJ

A finance function run with institutional discipline.

DSF Advisors is an outsourced CFO and COO partner for emerging asset managers, real estate investors, family offices, and professional services firms — the firms that need institutional-grade finance long before they can justify an institutional payroll.

We operate as an extension of your leadership team: fractional CFO leadership, accounting and reporting, fund operations, tax, and SEC compliance — engaged exactly to the scope you need, and scaled as you grow.

From day-one bookkeeping through to CFO-level strategy, DSF now covers the full financial stack — so you never need to coordinate between vendors, and nothing gets lost in translation.

"We carry the financial complexity so your attention stays where it belongs — on capital and clients."

03 What we do

Seven disciplines, run with institutional rigor.

Engage one or all seven. Each runs with the precision of an in-house team and the perspective of an outside partner who has done it before.

01

Fractional CFO Services

A seasoned financial lead who reads your firm's posture clearly and hands you the numbers to make decisions with conviction.

  • Review and approve fund admin financial packages
  • Strategic business planning, financial analysis, budgets & projections
  • Fund-level waterfall, carry, management & incentive fee calculations
  • Manage vendor relationships: fund admin, auditor, tax, legal, insurance
  • Cash management — bill pay, capital calls, distributions & payroll
  • Investor relations, due diligence, ad hoc requests & investor letters
  • Fund performance estimates & track record maintenance (GIPS)
  • Insurance procurement & employee payroll benefits setup
02

Fund Operations

Manager and fund set-up built to best practice from day one — so operations never become the thing that slows your raise.

  • Fund formation, tax structure & operating documents (LPA/PPM)
  • Operational setup: auditors, legal counsel, fund admins & compliance firms
  • Prime brokerage account establishment, including new international markets
  • Fund reporting, audit management & tax compliance coordination
  • Trade settlement & investor relations management
03

Accounting & Reporting

Clean, defensible books built to the standard your investors, auditors, and lenders expect.

  • Fund and management company accounting & financial reporting
  • Portfolio and project-level accounting
  • Financial statement preparation, including Shadow NAV
  • Institutional client reporting: SMAs and EMOMs
04

Back Office Support

The work that costs you trust when it's wrong, and disappears completely when it's right.

  • Trade processing and settlement support
  • Corporate actions & proxy management
  • Custody reconciliations & custodian communication
  • Shadow NAV preparation and reporting
  • Portfolio attribution & turnover calculations
  • Broker commission reporting
  • OMS/EMS provider selection & implementation
  • New investor and SMA onboarding
  • Fund presentations & marketing materials assistance
05

Tax Services

K-1 issuance, entity structuring, and year-round planning that keeps you ahead of every deadline.

Our tax practice has expanded — with the acquisition of Advisors CPA, clients now have a dedicated tax team and a full resource library. Explore the tax library →
  • Federal, state & local partnership tax returns and K-1 issuance
  • Tax advisory: manager/fund structuring (domestic & international)
  • Entity formation, partnership agreements & compliance requirements
  • Foreign filings & quarterly tax estimates
  • Fund-level tax compliance, including ASC 740 analysis
06

Regulatory Compliance

So an SEC examination is a process you're ready for — not an event that stops your firm.

  • SEC compliance program monitoring (CCO-directed)
  • Form ADV preparation & completion assistance
  • SEC audit request support for fund managers
  • Regulatory inquiry response & reporting
07

Bookkeeping & Accounting

Clean, current books are the foundation everything else rests on. DSF now provides full-service bookkeeping and accounting — transaction recording, reconciliations, payables, and month-end close — so your financial data is always accurate before it reaches any report, model, or investor.

  • Daily transaction recording and categorization
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Accounts payable & receivable management
  • Month-end and year-end close
  • General ledger maintenance
  • Payroll processing and benefits tracking
  • Financial statement preparation (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
  • Coordination with CPA for tax filings

Who we are

A firm built on the conviction that small firms deserve institutional finance.

DSF Advisors was founded in 2020 with a straightforward premise: emerging asset managers, real estate investors, family offices, and professional services firms need institutional-grade financial leadership long before they can justify the headcount to hire it full-time.

We are an outsourced CFO and COO partner — not a bookkeeper, not a consultant, and not a software platform. We are the finance function: we show up, we take ownership, and we carry the complexity that would otherwise fall on the founder.

"The firms we serve are too serious to cut corners on finance. We make sure they never have to."
Our approach

Institutional rigor. Boutique accountability.

We work as an extension of your leadership team — not as an outside vendor you have to manage. Every engagement is led directly by a senior principal, every deliverable is reviewed at the top, and every client gets the same standard of care regardless of size.

We are deliberately selective about who we take on, so the quality of our work is never diluted by volume. When you engage DSF, you are not handed off to a junior associate — you work with the people who built the firm.

Precision

Financial work that holds up under audit, investor scrutiny, and the hardest questions.

Ownership

We take the work off your plate — fully — rather than advising from the sidelines.

Transparency

No surprises in scope, billing, or findings. You always know where things stand.

Scalability

We grow with you — adding disciplines and depth as your obligations widen.

Additionally, Wilfredo helped lead the M&A transaction and led the full integration process with the Ashmore Group, a London-based public investment manager that acquired a majority stake in Avenida Capital.

Previously, Wilfredo was an Audit Manager at PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) for eight years, building the Big Four audit and accounting discipline that still defines how DSF works today.
