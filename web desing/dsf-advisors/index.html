01 About the firm
2020
Founded · Hackensack NJ

A finance function run with institutional discipline.

DSF Advisors is an outsourced CFO and COO partner for emerging asset managers, real estate investors, family offices, and professional services firms — the firms that need institutional-grade finance long before they can justify an institutional payroll.

We operate as an extension of your leadership team: fractional CFO leadership, accounting and reporting, fund operations, tax, and SEC compliance — engaged exactly to the scope you need, and scaled as you grow.

03 What we do

Six disciplines, run with institutional rigor.

Engage one or all six. Each runs with the precision of an in-house team and the perspective of an outside partner who has done it before.

01

Fractional CFO Services

A seasoned financial lead who reads your firm's posture clearly and hands you the numbers to make decisions with conviction.

  • Review and approve fund admin financial packages
  • Strategic business planning, financial analysis, budgets & projections
  • Fund-level waterfall, carry, management & incentive fee calculations
  • Manage vendor relationships: fund admin, auditor, tax, legal, insurance
  • Cash management — bill pay, capital calls, distributions & payroll
  • Investor relations, due diligence, ad hoc requests & investor letters
  • Fund performance estimates & track record maintenance (GIPS)
  • Insurance procurement & employee payroll benefits setup
02

Fund Operations

Manager and fund set-up built to best practice from day one — so operations never become the thing that slows your raise.

  • Fund formation, tax structure & operating documents (LPA/PPM)
  • Operational setup: auditors, legal counsel, fund admins & compliance firms
  • Prime brokerage account establishment, including new international markets
  • Fund reporting, audit management & tax compliance coordination
  • Trade settlement & investor relations management
03

Accounting & Reporting

Clean, defensible books built to the standard your investors, auditors, and lenders expect.

  • Fund and management company accounting & financial reporting
  • Portfolio and project level accounting
  • Financial statement preparation, including Shadow NAV
  • Institutional client reporting: SMAs and EMOMs
04

Back Office Support

The work that costs you trust when it's wrong, and disappears completely when it's right.

  • Trade processing and settlement support
  • Corporate actions & proxy management
  • Custody reconciliations & custodian communication
  • Shadow NAV preparation and reporting
  • Portfolio attribution & turnover calculations
  • Broker commission reporting
  • OMS/EMS provider selection & implementation
  • New investor and SMA onboarding
  • Fund presentations & marketing materials assistance
05

Tax Services

K-1 issuance, entity structuring, and year-round planning that keeps you ahead of every deadline.

  • Federal, state & local partnership tax returns and K-1 issuance
  • Tax advisory: manager/fund structuring (domestic & international)
  • Entity formation, partnership agreements & compliance requirements
  • Foreign filings & quarterly tax estimates
  • Fund-level tax compliance, including ASC 740 analysis
06

Regulatory Compliance

So an SEC examination is a process you're ready for — not an event that stops your firm.

  • SEC compliance program monitoring (CCO-directed)
  • Form ADV preparation & completion assistance
  • SEC audit request support for fund managers
  • Regulatory inquiry response & reporting
