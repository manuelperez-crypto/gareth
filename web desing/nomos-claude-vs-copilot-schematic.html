Claude vs Copilot

The difference, why enterprise adoption lags, and how safe it is — at a glance.

Claude vs Copilot
Model vs Product
01 What they are The core difference
  • Claude = the AI model — Anthropic builds the intelligence itself
  • Copilot = the product / wrapper — Microsoft's assistant inside Office & GitHub
  • Copilot is multi-model: it runs GPT · Claude · Gemini (you pick the engine)
  • Analogy: Claude is the engine, Copilot is the car
  • Not rivals — Copilot now runs Claude underneath as a selectable model
02 Why adoption lags "why nobody uses it"
  • Only ~3% pay of Microsoft 365 users buy Copilot (≈15M of 450M)
  • Of licensed users, only ~36% active — the rest is paid & idle
  • $30 / user / mo → ROI hard to prove, pilots stall for months
  • Governance fear: 40% delayed rollout 3+ months (Gartner)
  • Cause = ROI + data-governance fear, not weak AI
03 How safe it is Risks & reality
  • EchoLeak (CVE-2025-32711, 9.3 / 10) → zero-click prompt injection; patched, no known abuse
  • Oversharing → Copilot surfaces messy SharePoint permissions (~802K files at risk / org avg)
  • These are structural LLM risks (any AI + broad data) — not "Copilot is spyware"
  • Microsoft does not train on your tenant data; offers governance tooling
  • Anthropic / Claude: Constitutional AI · SOC 2 · ISO 27001 · HIPAA-ready · no training on enterprise data
04 Bottom line Takeaway
  • Difference: Claude = the intelligence · Copilot = the workplace wrapper
  • "Nobody uses it": overstated — but adoption is genuinely lagging
  • Safety: enterprise-safe if you clean up permissions & pick the right tier
  • Same class of risk for every provider — it scales with how much data you connect
