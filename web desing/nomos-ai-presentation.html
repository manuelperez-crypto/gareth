01 · The motive

Why do companies use AI?

01
Cost reduction
AI automates repetitive knowledge work — drafting, summarizing, extracting and classifying data — that once required teams of analysts. Up to 40% lower operating cost.
02
Decision speed
Compresses analysis cycles from days to minutes. Reports, summaries and documents that took a week are now produced in hours.
03
Competitive pressure
When competitors adopt AI and cut costs, those who don't fall behind. It is no longer optional — it's a baseline requirement to stay competitive.
04
Customer experience
Assistants that handle inquiries, route tickets and personalize communication at scale — cutting response times from hours to seconds.
05
Regulatory compliance
Monitors transactions, flags anomalies and drafts compliance reports — reducing the manual burden of audits and regulatory reporting.
06
Data intelligence
Companies sit on unstructured data — contracts, emails, transcripts. AI extracts patterns and risk signals that stay invisible to a human analyst.

08 · The reason

Why they choose Anthropic when the stakes are high

  • i
    Constitutional AI
    Principles built into the model: far less likely to invent citations, figures or false data — critical in financial and legal reports.
  • ii
    200K-token context
    Processes ~150,000 words in a single prompt without "forgetting" the middle. A 300-page memorandum fits whole.
  • iii
    Privacy by default
    Client data is not used to train future models — a legal necessity for banks, law firms and hospitals.
  • iv
    Calibrated uncertainty
    Says "I'm not sure, verify the source" instead of inventing. In compliance, that beats a confident but wrong answer.
  • v
    Instruction-following precision
    Formats, tables and calculations exactly as requested — fewer human correction loops in production.
